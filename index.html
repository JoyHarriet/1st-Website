A Trusted Partner for Global Recruitment

Skyways Global Services Ltd is a Nairobi-based human resource consultancy focused on connecting talent with employment opportunities across borders. We work with employers and job seekers to provide recruitment, candidate sourcing, documentation support, travel consultancy, visa assistance and pre-departure preparation. Our approach is built around professionalism, communication and helping candidates and employers navigate the recruitment journey efficiently.

Professional Recruitment

We connect qualified candidates with employment opportunities based on their skills, experience and the requirements of our recruiting partners.

End-to-End Support

From the initial application through documentation, visa processing and travel preparation, our team helps candidates understand and navigate each stage of the process

International Opportunities

We support recruitment opportunities across international markets, including the Middle East and other destinations where suitable opportunities are available.

Are you registered to provide recruitment services?

: Skyways Global Services operates as a recruitment and human resource consultancy in Kenya. Candidates should always confirm the current regulatory status of any recruitment service before making payments or committing to an opportunity.

How do I apply for a job?

The application process usually starts with an Interview then depending on the work the process takes about four weeks.

How long do the contracts last?

Most of the work contracts usually last about two years.

What happens after I apply?

The process depends on the specific position and employer. Candidates may go through screening, interviews, document verification, employer selection and other required stages before deployment.

How long does the recruitment process take?

There is no single timeline for every position. Processing times can vary depending on the employer, country, position, documentation, visa requirements and other factors.
